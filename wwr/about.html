We are dedicated to providing the most thrilling and safe white water rafting experiences. Our mission is to connect people with nature through unforgettable river adventures.

History

Founded in 1995, our company started with just two rafts and a passion for the river. Over the decades, we have grown into the region's premier rafting outfitter, guiding thousands of adventurers safely through the rapids every summer.

From calm scenic floats to adrenaline-pumping Class IV rapids, our certified guides bring decades of river knowledge and a commitment to safety, ensuring every trip creates lifelong memories.
